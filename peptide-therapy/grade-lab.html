The verdict I'd give

A2 · B2 · C2 — glacier end to end, with C3's halo held in reserve

A2 because a chrome that measures identical in lightness and opposite in temperature reads as a mistake, not a system — and the red mark carries the cross-site identity better than the ivory ever did. B2 because it is the only candidate that keeps the dawn's depth (the sections still read as places, not gaps) while moving its temperature — and the scene's payoff becomes first light over ice, which is this page's story told in its own colour. C2 because the scene is the page's heart, and the heart should beat in the page's metal; but C3 is genuinely beautiful and your instinct about that white is right — if the steel line reads too quiet on the stage, the ✦ BHRT halo treatment is the upgrade path, not a compromise.

Whatever the picks: the one red, the burgundy, the rose and both inks stay by brand law, BG_B moves in lockstep with the dawn token, and every alpha on the live page gets re-measured on the new grounds before it ships — the printed ratios here cover the flat candidates only.

Swap notes — Built against peptide-therapy/index.html. A is three values per file (bar ground, hairline, footer ground) plus the recorded fork of the true-copy chrome. B is the token swap the round-7 handover maps (ivory→#F4F7FA · cream→#EAF0F5 · line→#D5DEE6 · gold→#7C93A8 · gold-deep→#54687C), the dawn token to the winning candidate, and BG_B in the scene script to the same RGB — two copies of one colour, both must move. C is the scene's GOLD/GOLD_DEEP constants and the .scene-beat.gold rule; judged as a composition, not find-and-replaced.
