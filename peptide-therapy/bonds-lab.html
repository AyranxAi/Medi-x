D itself sits at the top as the reference, because it is live on the page right now and every candidate should be judged against what is actually there. The bar at the foot re-colours every plate between the chains' gold and this page's steel: the one open question the grade left behind.

Accent

The accent question. Plate D's chains are literally gold — rgba(194,160,94) — and that is how it was picked and how it shipped. But round 9 remapped this page's --gold token to steel #7C93A8, so those chains are now the only true gold on the page. The bar above shows both readings across all seven plates. Gold is the brand's voice and the warmest thing left on a cold page; steel is consistent and disappears into the grade. Neither is obviously right, which is why it is a bar and not a decision.

How to read the plates. Every one carries the real hero copy at the real sizes over the real glacier grade — the multiply is a layer here exactly as it is baked into the shipped draw() — so what you judge is what the page would be. Scroll a plate fully into view before judging it: the canvases only run while they are on screen, and the copy plays its entrance once.

If one of these wins it replaces D in peptide-therapy/index.html, one engine for another, and D joins E and Aero in the lab. Chain counts fall on narrow viewports on every plate here, the same way they do on the shipped page — a fixed-length rod covers about four times the share of a phone screen that it covers at 1440.
