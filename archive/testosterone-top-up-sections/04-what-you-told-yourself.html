Every symptom tells us a story. Testing helps us see the bigger picture.

Every one of these is a fair explanation. All of them at once is a blood test.

  1. “Work’s been heavy this year.”No energy
  2. “I’m just getting older.”Lost strength
  3. “I need a better mattress.”Poor sleep
  4. “We’re both just tired.”Low libido
  5. “Everyone’s stressed, it’s normal.”Burnout
  6. “I’ll get back to the gym properly.”Soft middle
  7. “I’m not angry, I’m busy.”Short temper
  8. “It’s just my father’s hairline.”Thinning hair

One symptom is a story. A pattern tells us more — and that pattern can be measured.
