Topping up is easy. Doing it safely is the art of precision medicine.

Anyone can raise a number. What makes this medical care is what happens next: your treatment is monitored — four key markers reviewed at every check-in, each helping us keep track of a different aspect of your health.

01

Testosterone, total

Are you in your range?

The number the whole programme is aimed at — and the one that says whether the dose is right, not merely working.

02

Haematocrit

How thick is your blood?

Testosterone tells the body to make more red cells. Left unwatched, that is the side effect that actually matters.

03

Estradiol

Where is it converting?

Men need estrogen too — some testosterone becomes it. Too much or too little can affect how you feel — the reason your treatment is monitored and adjusted.

04

PSA

Is the prostate quiet?

A baseline before you start and a check while you are on it — standard care, and the reason a prescriber is involved at all.

Your doctor may ask for more than these four, depending on your medical history, conditions and testing results. Any appointment beyond the first two-month phase is a new review consultation — so the review is a real one, not a rubber stamp.
