What are hormones?

Hormones are your body’s messengers, carrying signals from one organ to another. Your entire hormone family shapes much of the story — and, when needed, can be replaced with molecules identical to those your body naturally produces.

the estradiol your body makes the estradiol we prescribe

Identical. Not similar. Bioidentical means the same molecule your body already makes — plant-derived, then matched atom for atom. Your receptors can’t tell the difference. That’s the point.
