## 3 · The lesson this session cost the most to learn

⚠️⚠️ **THE RENDERED SLAB IS NOT THE TOKEN, AND A SWATCH WILL LIE TO YOU.** The flower's
scene lighting lifts and desaturates whatever the material is given: `#4E1A28` leaves as
`#5F3D43`.

The first attempt at per-door slabs kept the shipped burgundy's darkness and rotated only
the hue. Every candidate looked distinct as a hex. On the page the rendered slab moved by
**eight of 255** — and he said, correctly, that he could not see a difference.

**Saturation and lightness survive the lighting; hue alone does not.** `returning-band.mjs`
§7c now samples the painted canvas and requires the three doors to separate by **≥30** —
the check that first attempt would have failed.

## 5 · CANCELLED, and the work so it is not redone

He asked for the menopause pink **deeper**, then cancelled before it shipped. **Nothing was
committed** — `main` carries `#C9366C`. The research, in full:

⚠️ **DEEPENING THIS PINK WALKS IT INTO BHRT's CRIMSON.** They are neighbours in hue (326 vs
348), so lightness alone cannot buy depth. Measured, rendered, against the crimson's
`rgb(169,64,78)`:

| candidate | spec | renders as | Δ vs crimson | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| live now | `#C9366C` | `rgb(208,76,117)` | 56 | ✓ |
| deepened, hue held at 333 | `#AD1F5F` | `rgb(181,63,106)` | **30** | at the floor |
| deepened further, hue 330 | `#A0185C` | `rgb(168,60,104)` | **26** | ✗ **FAILS** |
| **deep, hue moved to 326** | **`#AD1A6D`** | `rgb(181,61,118)` | **42** | ✓ the pick |
| deep, hue 320 | `#A81577` | `rgb(176,59,126)` | 49 | ✓ magenta lean |
| deep, hue 314 | `#A51283` | `rgb(173,58,137)` | 59 | reads fuchsia, rejected |

**The finding: separation does not have to come from lightness.** Pushing the hue away from
red buys the room to go deep and hold distance. `#AD1A6D` (hue 326) is the same depth as
the hue-333 attempt but sits 42 from the crimson instead of 30.

**To ship it** — four tokens on `/modern-menopause/`, one expectation in the harness:

```
--ps-slab:#AD1A6D;  --ps-slab-top:#DB298E;  --ps-slab-mid:#BB1674;  --ps-slab-foot:#9C0D5E;
returning-band.mjs §7c: 'modern-menopause': { spec: '#AD1A6D', rend: [181, 61, 118] }
```

Verified green before it was reverted. ⚠️ **If it is ever deepened again, the hue must move
with it** — or the two doors stop being tellable apart, which is the one thing the theming
exists to do.
