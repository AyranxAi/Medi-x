The orrery, rendered.

The concept stays — five systems on five tilts around one nucleus, letter F, his own pick. What is on trial here is the rendering, which never left wireframe: flat 1px strokes, a grey that belongs to no part of the palette, and no ring passing in front of any other. Nine plates, one seeded universe (424242), the live file's tilts and speeds throughout.

Why it reads as pencil

  1. Every stroke is lineWidth = 1. Depth is carried by alpha alone (.14 → .30). Uniform hairline weight is the definition of a technical drawing — weight-varies-with-depth is the strongest 2.5D cue available and it is unused.
  2. The ring colour is grey. rgba(106,88,95,·) is --ink-soft; at 14–30% over ivory it lands near #E4DDDA–#CFC4C4 — desaturated warm grey, which is graphite. It is also the only hue in the hero that belongs to no part of the palette.
  3. Nothing occludes anything. Rings are drawn one whole ring at a time, so none of them ever passes in front of another or in front of the nucleus. Five ellipses printed on one plane read as a spirograph, not as a system.
  4. On ivory, gold cannot get brighter — only more saturated. The ground is at ~97% luminance, so there is no headroom above the paper for a light source to occupy. This is the arithmetic reason the hero reads dead, and it is why the plates that work all either deepen the ground under the orrery or move it onto a dark plate.
  5. The composition is polite. The whole universe fits inside the canvas with room to spare, centred at 70% width and 48% height. Nothing crops, so nothing implies it continues past the frame.
  6. The lights are 1.8–2.9px dots. Against a 100px headline there is no accent at the right-hand end of the composition with enough mass to answer it.

E · Aperture — depth of field Ember's palette, plus the cue no diagram ever has: the far side is out of focus. H · Aurum — everything at once, on ivory Ember's palette + Aperture's defocus + Ecliptic's crop and dust plate + a specular highlight on the nucleus + the widest warm pool. If a single plate is going to be called award-grade it is this one — and it is also the one to argue with, because it spends every device at once. Move the pointer: the whole universe answers by 14px. That parallax is on every plate here, and it is off under reduced motion.

What to do with this

The cheapest real fix is A. Two changes — a depth-ramped stroke width and one global z-sort — and the pencil is gone without a single hue moving. If nothing else is agreed, that one lands.

The recommendation is B, with E's defocus folded in. Ember gives the hero a reason for its own colour: the ring warms as it approaches because the light is at the centre. That is an argument, not a decoration, and it is the same argument the page makes in words two screens down.

⚠️ Whichever plate is picked lands in two places. The scroll scene below the fold is the hero's own universe by design (round 3 — "speak the hero's language twice"). Its generator, seed and tilts are a second copy of the hero's. Re-render one and the other must follow in the same commit.

⚠️ Ask the round-3 question of every plate before it ships: what else does this look like? The river scene passed every geometric check and still had to go, because branching channels with travelling particles is the grammar of a transmission map. An orrery's own nearest wrong reading is an atom — which is why the tilts stay irregular and the radii stay uneven on every plate here. Regularise them and it becomes a nuclear-industry logo.
