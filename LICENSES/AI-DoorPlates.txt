WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
--------------------
Same reason as CC0-VenusDeMilo-SMK.txt: so the next session does not have to ask where
a picture came from. This is a doctor-led medical site in a regulated market, and the
provenance of a photograph is part of whether it may be published, not a footnote.

NO IDENTIFIABLE PERSON APPEARS
------------------------------
Two of the three plates show a human figure. Both are framed above the face — the BHRT
plate has no figure at all. Nobody in them is identifiable, so no model release is
required and none exists. This is load-bearing, not a coincidence of the crop:

  ⚠️ Do not re-crop these plates to include a face, and do not replace them with a
     frame that has one, without a signed model release. A recognisable face on a
     hormone-clinic page is an implied patient testimonial whether or not any words
     accompany it.

WHAT THE PICTURES SHOW
----------------------
A smart ring on a charging dock is visible in the Modern Menopause and Testosterone
Replacement plates. Kept as set dressing by the owner's call, 2026-08-12, after being
raised as a risk. It is worth restating the risk rather than burying it: on a
regulated medical page, a device in frame can read as a device the clinic supplies.
If Medi-X does not supply a wearable, the crops can be tightened to drop it — the
copy band would be unaffected — or the plates re-generated without it.

DISCLOSURE
----------
Nothing on the page states that the imagery is synthetic. That is normal for
decorative photography and these are decorative — every plate ships alt="" and
aria-hidden="true", and no plate is offered as evidence of a patient, a result, a
member of staff, or a facility. If any of them is ever reused in a context that
implies documentation rather than decoration, that changes and the page needs to say
so.
